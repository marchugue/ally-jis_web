🔍 Capstone Architecture Defense & Audit Notes

✅ Process 5.0: Administration & Moderation

Provides complete data loop closure: student user reports flow into D5 (Reports), which are fetched and resolved by Process 5.0 via the Admin Portal. Prevents the "black hole / orphan data" objection from panelists.

✅ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Accurately models dual-entry points: Student Mobile/Web clients communicate with 1.0 User Auth, while privileged staff authenticate via Admin RBAC (admin.ally-jis.xyz) with session claims verified against D1.

✅ Level 2 Subsystem Decomposition

Adheres to strict Gane & Sarson standards: Process 5.0 decomposes cleanly into 5.1 (Account Management & Bans), 5.2 (Report Triage & Chat Evidence), and 5.3 (Settings & Audit Logs).

⚠️ Passive Data Store Rule Compliance

In standard DFD rules, data stores (D1–D6) remain strictly passive. Communication between stores (e.g. evaluating chat logs during report triage) is always mediated through active processes (5.2 Incident Resolution).
